About Me

Research Master’s graduate in Data Science and Smart Services from ISTIC, University of Carthage. My research focuses on making AI-generated code secure: during my thesis I built a new prompt–code benchmark and designed HAGM-DFA, a graph neural network that detects vulnerabilities in code written by large language models, work that led to two research articles. I am now looking for a PhD position to pursue this research on trustworthy and secure AI.

Research Interests

AI Security · Graph Learning · Large Language Models

  • Security of code generated by large language models: understanding and detecting prompt-induced vulnerabilities.
  • Graph neural networks for program analysis (AST, control flow, data flow) combined with pretrained code models.
  • Reliable benchmarks and rigorous evaluation: label quality, LLM-as-a-Judge, leakage-free experimental design.
  • A strong engineering background (full-stack and AI) to turn research ideas into working prototypes.
