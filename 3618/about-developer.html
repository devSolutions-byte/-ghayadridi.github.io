About Me

AI practitioner with a Master’s degree in Data Science (highest honours) and a Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from ISTIC, University of Carthage. I use ChatGPT and other LLMs in real projects: as a freelancer I integrated the OpenAI API into an ERP to generate reports automatically, I built a question-answering chatbot with LangChain and a multi-step AI agent with LangGraph, and I automate workflows with n8n. In my Master’s research I used LLMs at scale (11,995 generated programs, automatic labeling by LLM judges) and studied their limits, in particular the security flaws they can introduce. I like to explain AI simply and show how to use it in everyday work, with concrete examples.
